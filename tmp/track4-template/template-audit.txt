Track 4 template audit

Slides inspected: all 39 source slides.

Visual system

- Light slides use white-to-pale-blue gradients; dark slides use navy-to-mid-blue gradients.
- Body type: Inter Tight. Secondary technical type: IBM Plex Mono.
- Top-right BlendED wordmark is inherited from the template master/layout system.

- Slide 11: dark half-image + large metric/title.
- Slide 12: dark title/subtitle + two picture slots.
- Slide 14: light title + large subtitle/body canvas.
- Slide 16: light title + six compact paired text slots.

Inherited placeholders
- Every mapped source slide uses structural title/subtitle/body/picture placeholders.
- Each placeholder is rewritten or replaced through its inherited shape id.
- Decorative template shapes and inherited BlendED branding remain untouched.

Insertion contract
- Output slides are duplicated from the mapped source slides.
- Existing placeholders are filled directly; no parallel design is overlaid on copied slides.
- Dashboard images replace inherited picture placeholders and preserve their frame geometry.
- Speaker notes retain internal project sources in [Sources] blocks.

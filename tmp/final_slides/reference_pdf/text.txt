CONCLUSION
AI is not simply "eliminating" jobs, but is "reshaping" tasks.
Currently, AI plays an uneven but critical role in the U.S. economy: it
functions primarily as an amplifying force, especially in high-
intellect, high-growth sectors. Future career resilience will no
longer depend on avoiding AI, but on leveraging AI as a multiplier
of professional competitiveness through specialization and by
enhancing the ability to collaborate with AI.
